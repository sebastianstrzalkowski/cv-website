## Platform

web

## Users

Four confirmed audiences, in priority order:

1. **Recruiters and hiring managers** evaluating Sebastian for a senior DLT / blockchain or Java backend role. Their job: judge seniority and fit quickly, then download the CV or send an email.
2. **Clients seeking contract or consulting work**: companies and founders who need a blockchain architect or advisor on a project basis. Their job: verify production credibility, then make contact.
3. **Conference and community organizers** booking speakers or mentors. Their job: see past talks, media appearances and topics, then reach out.
4. **Peers and the Web3 community** who found Sebastian via a talk, YouTube or GitHub and want to connect or follow.

All audiences arrive cold, usually from LinkedIn, a conference, or a search, and decide within one scroll whether to keep reading. The site is read in English or Polish; i18next falls back to English, while date formatting in the data layer falls back to Polish.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and CV site for Sebastian Strzałkowski, Senior DLT Engineer & Blockchain Architect. It presents career history, side projects, talks, media, trainings and contact channels in one place, and ships a printable A4 CV directly from the page. Success means a visitor contacts Sebastian or takes the CV, having understood what he is credible for.

## Positioning

Enterprise banking DLT delivered to production. Sebastian has shipped a flagship institutional Tokenised Deposits platform for a global Tier-1 bank (24/7 real-time interbank settlement, treasury liquidity, tokenized RWA/MMF workflows) on Hyperledger and Kaleido, integrated with wholesale CBDC rails and Fireblocks custody. This sits on top of eight-plus years of enterprise Java / Spring distributed-systems engineering (Inetum, 2018–2025) and public-chain Solidity work. Most senior blockchain portfolios can claim DeFi or NFT work; few can claim regulated banking DLT in production.

Secondary, confirmed supporting claims: active conference speaker and Web3 mentor in the Polish scene; combined on-chain protocol design with cloud and distributed-systems depth (AWS, Terraform, Flink, Reactor).

## Operating Context

- Visitors typically come from LinkedIn, conference follow-ups, YouTube talks or recruiter searches, on desktop or phone.
- The CV is consumed as an A4 print or PDF export from the in-page modal; recruiters forward it inside ATS systems and email.
- Contact happens through outbound links (email, Telegram and LinkedIn cards; GitHub and Discord handle below; mailto, GitHub and LinkedIn in the footer). The phone number is deliberately not shown on the site; it appears only in the CV. EmailJS is installed and a form's copy exists in both locales, but no form is rendered today.
- Deployment: static Vite build synced to S3 and served through CloudFront by GitHub Actions on push.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Live at https://sebastian.i-can-do.it (CloudFront behind Cloudflare). The canonical URL, Open Graph tags and sitemap are generated from VITE_SITE_URL in vite.config.js.

Confirmed functionality:

- Sections: Hero, About (skills, specializations, additional tools, education), Experience, Projects, Media (YouTube talks), Conferences, Trainings (with certificates), Contact, Footer.
- Bilingual EN/PL through i18next with browser language detection and a manual switcher. **Parity is mandatory**: every section, label and data string exists in both languages.
- CV preview modal with print / save-as-PDF and open-in-new-tab. The CV HTML lives in `src/data/cvHtml.js` and is generated by `generate_cv.cjs`; it is a core feature, not decoration. A static PDF copy also ships at `public/CV_Sebastian_Strzalkowski.pdf`.
- Years of experience is computed from July 2018 at runtime; never hard-code it.
- Scroll-spy navigation, scroll progress bar, back-to-top. A contact form is not rendered; its translations and the EmailJS dependency are present but unused.

Technical constraints:

- Stack: React 18, Vite 6, Tailwind CSS 3, Framer Motion 12, lucide-react, i18next. Single-page app with one `index.html` shell.
- All product data (experience, projects, media, conferences, trainings, contact) is centralized in `src/data/developerData.js` with per-language getters; UI copy is in `src/locales/{en,pl}/translation.json`.
- Static hosting only (S3 + CloudFront); no server-side rendering or backend.

Terminology: DLT (distributed ledger technology), RWA (real-world assets), MMF (money market funds), CBDC, EVM, L2 rollups, Hyperledger, Kaleido BaaS, Fireblocks, Tatum.

Undecided product facts:

- Whether the dark-only theme is a binding commitment or open to change was not confirmed; treat the current dark theme as incumbent, not as a rule.
- No stated preference on which audience's call to action leads the Hero when they conflict.

## Product Principles

1. **Production credibility leads.** Regulated banking DLT shipped to production is the differentiator; surface it before generic Web3 claims.
2. **Fast judgment for cold visitors.** Every audience should know within one viewport what Sebastian does and how to reach him.
3. **Both languages, always.** A change that lands in one locale is incomplete.
4. **The CV is a product surface.** The print/PDF flow must keep working and reflect the same data as the site.
5. **Facts over flourish.** Content stays specific, verifiable and sourced from `developerData.js`; no invented proof.
